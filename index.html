Privacy Policy for Escape Vault

Effective Date: October 2, 2026

Escape Vault ("we", "our", or "the app") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how Escape Vault handles information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Access

Escape Vault may access certain information from your device only when it is required to provide the features of the application.

Contacts

The app may request access to your contacts so that you can select a contact number for the fake call or related simulation features. Contact information used by the app is processed locally on your device and is not transmitted to our servers.

Device Storage and Settings

Escape Vault may store selected app settings and user-provided information locally on your device using Android SharedPreferences. We do not operate a backend server or database for storing this information.

2. App Permissions

Depending on the features you use, Escape Vault may request permissions or access related to:

  • Contacts — to allow you to select contact information for simulated calls.
  • Notifications — to provide notifications related to app features.
  • Vibration — to provide vibration effects for simulated events.
  • Wake Lock — to keep required app functionality active when necessary.
  • Exact Alarm — to schedule simulated events at the time selected by the user.
  • Foreground Service — to support certain background features such as scheduled fake calls.
  • Full-Screen Intent — to display certain simulated incoming-call experiences.

These permissions are used only for the functionality provided by Escape Vault.

3. Fake Call, Fake SMS and Fake Shutdown

Escape Vault provides simulated entertainment features such as fake calls, fake SMS conversations, and fake shutdown screens. These features are simulations inside the application and are not intended to send real SMS messages, make unauthorized phone calls, or shut down your device.

4. Advertising

Escape Vault uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and process certain information, such as device identifiers, advertising-related information, and other information required to provide and measure advertisements, according to Google's policies.

For more information about how Google handles data, please review Google's Privacy Policy.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties through our own servers.

However, third-party services such as Google AdMob may process information as described in their own privacy policies.

6. Data Security

Information stored locally by Escape Vault remains on your device and is not transmitted to our backend because the application does not operate a backend server or database.

7. Children's Privacy

Escape Vault is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through our own servers.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page by updating the effective date.
